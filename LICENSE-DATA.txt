Underlying content
------------------
The compilation records a public municipal process. The proposal titles in
`data/ballot.json` were written by participants in MünchenBudget and published
by the City of Munich at https://unser.muenchen.de/. No rights in that text are
claimed or granted here: it is reproduced as factual metadata about a public
participatory-budgeting round, and any rights in it remain with its authors and
with the City of Munich. The same reservation applies to any third-party wording
quoted in `data/consul_sort_audit/`.

Reusers who rely on that underlying text, rather than on the factual record
compiled around it, should satisfy themselves as to its status.


Personal data
-------------
The distributed files contain no author names, no user identifiers and no
comment text. See `data/README.md` for provenance, and for the author-derived
material that is deliberately not distributed with this package.
