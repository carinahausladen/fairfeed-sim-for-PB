Interactive companion to Fair Feed Ranking for Participatory Budgeting (GoodIT '26)

Who gets seen decides what gets funded

In large participatory budgets, hundreds of proposals compete for citizens' support, and only a few reach the final vote. Nobody reads them all, so the order of the list shapes the ballot. This page runs the simulation from the paper in your browser. Change the rules and watch what happens.

The paper's results, recomputed as you play

These charts rerun the paper's experiments for all four feeds with your settings. As in the paper, (a) and (b) let citizens stop browsing when the feed turns irrelevant; (c) and (d) hold browsing fixed, so any difference comes from the order alone.

(a) How far citizens read

Cards browsed per citizen, and how many of them match the citizen's topic.

(b) Who gets seen

Share of proposals seen at least a given number of times.

(c) Voting beyond one's own proposal

Each citizen arrives for one proposal. How many others do they support?

(d) How good is the ballot?

Hidden quality of the 20 ballot proposals. Dots: one run. Bars: average over runs.
